title: Speed readouts
status: ready
slots: label-cycle-estimate
note: UX 6.2 and 8.5, one card per detent, in knob order (RUN, 1/30, 1/300, 1/3000, CRAWL, STEP). text is the readout under the knob; meta is the lamp caption on The Computer (PANEL-3). The detent names and the ratios come from the engine's calibration record, not from here.

## run
text: Full speed. The computer checks the keys thousands of times a second.
meta: The lamps show the address of the instruction now running. At full speed they flicker too fast to read.

## div30
text: A thirtieth of the speed. Notes still sound at once. Listen to the edges.
meta: A little slower. The lamps still blur, but the pattern has a beat.

## div300
text: Now you can hear it. Each note arrives a moment late.
meta: Slower again. You can see the loop sweep round.

## div3000
text: Slow enough to hear it think. Every note waits for its lap of the loop.
meta: The loop is sweeping. Slow it more, or press STEP, to read it.

## crawl
text: Crawling. Watch the code, one instruction at a time. Notes take many seconds.
meta: Crawling. The lamps follow the program a few instructions a second.

## step
text: Stopped. Press STEP to run one instruction.
meta: Stopped. The lamps show exactly where the computer is waiting. Press STEP.

## estimate
text: Speeds are our estimate until the 7/16's own timing table is found.
